Computer aided mathematics and visualization

  • Subject codeINBPA0103-17
  • Responsible personDr. Kunkli Roland Imre
  • Credits6
  • Lessons/week2+0+2
  • TypeCompulsory
  • AssessmentPractical mark

Description

Functions, relations, basic function types. Drawing function graphs with computer software, visualizing different function properties. Bivariate functions and the possibilities of the visualization of their graphs. Basic vector operations, multiplication of vectors. Introducing derivatives and integrals through interactive and illustrative visual examples. Implicit equations and parametric equation systems of curves and surfaces. Short review of other well-known techniques for visualizing surfaces. Equations and equation systems of lines and planes. Relationships among spatial objects, distance and angle measurement. Finite mathematical and geometrical problems, and their computer based solutions. Matrices (multiplication of matrices, inverse, determinant). Linear equation systems through a geometrical approach. Linear transformations, orthogonal and symmetric matrices. Interesting problems solved by using homogeneous coordinates.

Competences

  • knowledges

    • be familiar with general and specific mathematical and computational theories, facts, rules, relations and procedures serving as a basis of their specialization, such as analysis (calculus), discrete mathematics and linear algebra
    • possess specific knowledge of tools essential for selecting them and accomplishing simple tasks in the field of computer visualization

  • abilities

    • apply general and specific principles, facts, rules and relations of Mathematics and Informatics related to the field of their specialization
    • continuously develop their knowledge of Informatics on the acquired basis of the principles, facts, rules and procedures of Mathematics and Informatics

  • attitude

    • aim to develop their own knowledge by means of continuous professional training and self-tuition

  • autonomy, responsibility

    • take responsibility for accomplishing minor tasks in complex software development projects
    • take responsibility for their professional activities
    • aim to accomplish quality work efficiently

Detailed subject matter

Lecture

Instructors

Dr. Kunkli Roland Imre

Weekly schedule

  1. Vectors.
  2. Dot product. Cross product.
  3. Explicit equations. Interpolation with polynomial functions.
  4. Implicit and parametric curves.
  5. Interpolation with parametric curves.
  6. Derivatives. Hermite curves.
  7. Bézier curves.
  8. Explicit and implicit surfaces.
  9. Parametric surfaces.
  10. 2D and 3D transformations using matrices.
  11. Mappings from 3D to 2D.
  12. Points, lines, and planes. Relationships among spatial objects. Distance and angle measurement.
  13. The foundations of projective geometry. Homogeneous coordinates.
